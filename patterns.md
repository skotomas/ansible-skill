# Pattern selection

Use [roles and reuse](chapters/ch08-roles.md) for component boundaries, [inventory](chapters/ch03-inventory.md) for environments, [control flow](chapters/ch07-control-flow.md) for rolling changes, [secrets](chapters/ch10-vault-secrets.md) for credential choices, and [validation](chapters/ch13-testing-validation.md) for tests.

Choose a pattern to solve a concrete requirement. Roles, Vault, Terraform, Molecule and AWX are not universal prerequisites. Keep operational guidance in the corresponding chapter to avoid conflicting duplicate rules.
